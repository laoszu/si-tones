## Challenges

### Diglossia
Co-existence of multiple language varieties within a single country:
- regional accents with distinct vocabulary and local variants
- co-existence of vernacular languages with the official standard (Putonghua)
- [Regional Accent Survey (arxiv)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2406.03814)
